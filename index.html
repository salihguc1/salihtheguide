About me

Muhammed Salih Güç

I've been guiding in Istanbul for four years, and I still find something new on every walk.

My tours are relaxed and personal. We take the backstreets between the big sights, stop for tea where locals drink it, and talk about the city's 2,700 years of history in a way that actually sticks.

I guide in English and Turkish, and I'm happy to adapt every tour for families, slower walkers or first-time visitors.
